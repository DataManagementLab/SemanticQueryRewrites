You are encouraged to produce semantically non-equivalent queries (when your rules are applied to the original query) as long as it remains valid on this Visual Genome dataset and preserves the intent. Optimization focus
- Only apply shortcuts that are realistic for this Visual Genome dataset and likely to speed up queries. You may not drop rare/edge cases to simplify the plan.
- Do not invent tables/columns. Use exactly the column names from the schema (they are case-sensitive and used quoted).
- Prefer sargable predicates (e.g. range bounds) that the optimizer can use to prune scans.

Scene-graph-specific, world-knowledge-driven shortcuts you should consider when applicable
- Image carry: IMG_OBJ, IMG_OBJ_ATT and IMG_REL share IMG_ID; an image filter on one side holds on the joined side.
- Object-sample carry: attributes and relations reference OBJ_SAMPLE_ID within an image — pin the object on both sides of the join.
- Bounding-box sanity: IMG_OBJ X/Y/W/H are non-negative and bounded by the image dimensions; a width/height filter implies a coordinate range.
- Class consistency: an OBJ_CLASS_ID / ATT_CLASS_ID / PRED_CLASS_ID filter maps to exactly one class label in the lookup tables.
- Think of more and other filters that can be applied, depending on the query's tables and filters.
