Basketball-specific, world-knowledge-driven shortcuts you should consider when applicable

- Conference validity: filtering on confID = 'EA'/'WE' implies the NBA modern era (year >= 1970).
- Postseason tables: joining series_post or using Post* columns implies the team/player reached the playoffs; you may add the matching playoff/league filters.
